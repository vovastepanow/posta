# Posta-Dataset-v0.1 research candidate — 21 November 2026

The local candidate is `/mnt/hdd_ubuntu/posta-pmr/Posta-Dataset-v0.1-screened`.
It is **quarantined, not cleared for model training**: the source terms,
individual media permissions, personal-information screening, near duplicates,
and overlap with independent benchmarks beyond MTEB RiaNews require review.
The source downloads and this corpus do not touch production PostgreSQL.

## Actual contents

The local split is 67,133 train, 3,474 validation and 3,695 test. These are
internal splits, not independent benchmarks. The corpus includes 13,930
referenced media files; source images and OCR were decoded during extraction,
video streams were fully decoded, and audio was decoded in an earlier candidate
with identical SHA-256 bytes. The final integrity check rehashed every referenced
file and found no missing or unreferenced media. Metadata include source
revisions, hashes, modality, language, source groups, annotation provenance,
OCR boxes, and source video times where available.

The 30 downloaded source files were SHA-256 checked by the downloader upon
completion. They are selected shards, **not full copies** of multi-terabyte
collections. MegaPairs is gated; the audited Common Voice mirror is empty.
DataComp-HQ contains URL metadata, not verified media. BGE-M3's first archive
entry contains retrieval data, but its component terms and independent
benchmark overlap were not resolved and it was not added to the candidate.
FineWeb2-HQ's upstream embeddings were not consumed.

The preliminary global merge rejected one malformed retrieval example and
removed 13,589 exact duplicates; a later merge removed another 236. A MTEB
RiaNews check found six distinct normalized-text matches. The screening pass
excluded 70 affected records (one web record and 69 OCR records). The repeated
check scanned 714,344 benchmark text rows and found **zero exact matches**.
Exact checks include queries, positives, negatives, OCR text and OCR annotation
text, supplementary captions and subtitles. They do not guarantee absence of
near duplicates, translations or contamination from other benchmarks, including
MMEB. Different versions sharing an exact source URL or FineWeb2 family ID
are assigned to the same internal split.

`manifest.json` contains source SHAs, license claims/review status and output
hashes. `validation.json`, `statistics.json`, `mteb-overlap.json`,
`benchmark-exclusions.jsonl`, `rejections.jsonl` and `duplicates.jsonl` document
the final pass; earlier pass reports remain in their parent candidate directories.
There are 5,315 retrieval examples without explicit negatives after safe
filtering; a future trainer must handle these explicitly. The text contains
25,546,970 whitespace-delimited words (17,768,744 Russian and 7,778,226 English),
not an exact tokenizer count. Video timestamp duration differences above one
second were not observed in this sample. These checks do not constitute human
annotation-quality or privacy review.

## Before model training

1. Review source and per-media rights, attribution, privacy, and mMARCO/MS MARCO
   usage restrictions; do not approve terms automatically.
2. Screen near duplicates, translated material and overlap against additional
   held-out benchmarks and independently designed Posta queries; review
   MemeXplain's empty upstream `source_split` field.
3. Decide how to handle the 5,315 positive-only retrieval records, expand topic
   and language diversity, and choose a reproducible tokenizer.
4. Only after these gates pass, design and initialize PMR's own model weights and
   run the first bounded training experiment. No model was trained here.
